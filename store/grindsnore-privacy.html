GrindSnore — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2024

The short version

GrindSnore does not collect any data, and no audio ever leaves your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

How recording works

  • Recording starts only when you tap Start
  • Sounds are analyzed on your phone, in real time
  • Short clips of detected events (snore / grind) are stored only on your device, so you can listen back and correct them — they are never uploaded
  • Your morning check-in answers, notes, and reports stay on your device

What we never do

  • We never upload, transmit, or sell anything
  • We never listen remotely or store audio on any server
  • Sharing happens only when you choose to share (export a report yourself)

Feedback

If you use in-app feedback, your message is sent by your own email app and contains only what you write (plus, if you corrected any detected events, a list of those corrections).

Not a medical device

GrindSnore records sounds and shows patterns. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. For concerns about snoring or teeth grinding (bruxism), consult your doctor or dentist.
